# Lead sources, markets, marketing, and tools

## Picking a market [FWR site topics + General]
Flip With Rick lessons: "What Market to Pick?" (market analysis playlist), "How to Pick the BEST & HOTTEST Virtual Wholesaling MARKETS!", "How to Find the Best Zip Codes for your Market". Their JV program wants counties of **50,000+ people** [FWR site]. General signals of a good wholesaling market: lots of cash sales and flips, older housing stock, prices affordable enough for investors, steady population, and enough buyers. Their AI prompt list includes market research questions like "What zip code in [city] has the most cash sales per thousand people?", "What zip codes have the largest concentration of older homes in [city]?", "What are the best zip codes for house flipping in [city]?" [FWR site].

## Lead sources Flip With Rick teaches [FWR site]
- **Zillow FSBO and agent listings** (see Zillow files) - free.
- **Driving for dollars (D4D)** - spotting distressed houses (overgrown, boarded, mail piling up) and looking up owners (XLeads/DealMachine D4D apps).
- **Pulling free lists / government lists** - county requests for **code violations**, **water shut-off** (non-payment), **tax delinquent**, **fire-damaged** properties, **probate** records, **pre-foreclosures**. Their trick: have ChatGPT write the public-records email, e.g. "Write up an email to request all open code violations for the past 30 days in [county], written from [your name]". Videos: "Exactly How To Pull County Lists in 60 Seconds (FREE)", "How to Pull The Water Shutoff List", "How to PULL & Wholesale Government LISTS for MASSIVE ASSIGNMENT FEES!".
- **List software**: PropStream ("How to Hack Propstream for the BEST Lists"), XLeads (their main tool), DealMachine.
- **Probates**, **pre-foreclosures**, **foreclosure auctions** ("How to Get Your First Foreclosure Wholesale Deal (Auction.com Method)"; 2026 videos on foreclosures surging), **tired landlords**, **vacant land**, **mobile homes**, **fire-damaged** houses.
- **Marketing channels**: cold calling, SMS text blasting, direct mail, Craigslist, Facebook groups, bandit signs, PPC. They teach "How to Make $25,000 a Month WITH NO COLD CALLING" as well.

## Skip tracing [FWR site]
Free: **TruePeopleSearch.com** and **CyberBackgroundChecks.com** (they call it "the most accurate free skiptracing"). Paid/bulk: XLeads (unlimited skip tracing with their plan).

## Daily schedule and consistency [FWR video topics + General]
Videos: "The PERFECT Daily Schedule for Wholesaling Real Estate Success", "Wholesaling Part-Time", "MY ADVICE If You Haven't Gotten Your First Deal Yet". General structure: block time for (1) new leads/list building, (2) calls and follow-ups (the bulk of the day), (3) comps and offers, (4) buyer building. Track dials -> conversations -> offers -> contracts weekly. Part-timers: 2 focused hours a day of calls and follow-ups beats sporadic marathons.
