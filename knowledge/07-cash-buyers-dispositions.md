## Where cash buyers come from [General + FWR topics]
- **Facebook** investor groups and the ad above.
- **Recently sold cash sales**: county records, PropStream/XLeads "cash buyer" filters, or Zillow sold listings that were flipped or turned into rentals (see Zillow file). Skip-trace the buyer LLC's owner.
- **Realtor cash buyers**: agents who keep listing flips represent investors; agents who are investors themselves.
- **Landlords**: owners of multiple rentals, property managers.
- **Other wholesalers and JV partners** (Flip With Rick's JV program splits 50/50 on off-market deals in 50k+ population counties [FWR site]).
- **Hard-money lenders and title companies**: they know who's buying.
- **Bandit signs / Craigslist / investor meetups**.
- Dispo platforms (e.g. XLeads Dispo [FWR site]).

## Build a buy box for each buyer [General]
Ask: areas/zip codes, price range, property types, beds/baths, max rehab level, flip or rental, how they pay (cash/hard money), how fast they close, and how many they bought in the last 6-12 months. Match deals to buy boxes instead of blasting everyone. Ideally line up buyers **before** you contract in a new market.

## Vetting buyers [General]
Look up their past purchases in county records. Ask for proof of funds or a lender letter. Require a **non-refundable deposit** with the assignment. Prefer buyers who have closed with your title company before.

## Marketing a deal [General]
Send a clean deal sheet: photos, address (or area until they commit), beds/baths/sq ft, year built, **asking (your buyer price)**, **ARV with 3 comps**, **repair estimate**, access/walkthrough times, closing date, and deposit required. Price it so the buyer still makes their margin (your buyer price = investor method's "buyer's max price" or a bit below). Run a walkthrough or open house to create urgency.

## Can't find a buyer? [FWR site topic + General]
Re-check your numbers (usually ARV too high or repairs too low), drop your fee, widen your buyer search, JV with a local wholesaler, or ask the seller for more time or a lower price based on real repair bids. If none of that works, cancel within your inspection period and tell the seller honestly. Don't let a deal drag past your contingency deadlines.
